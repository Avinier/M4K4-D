**How the model uses them:**
- **Sharp sensor:** the model trims both mounting ears to the 29.5 mm body, because the sensor sits in a 31 mm pocket and is clamped by the seat and lid (D-027). The STEP stays untrimmed.
- **Adafruit board:** regenerate the STEP with `python adafruit_3297_drv8833.py` (text-to-cad 0.4.28 runtime). `ADAFRUIT_3297_LEAVES` in the model relies on the script's export order.

**Still boxes:** the Robocraze TIFPS0629 protection module (PCB-01) stays a 48 × 20 × 2.9 mm placeholder. There is no drawing or model, the listing gives only the footprint, and the height is unmeasured. The custom boards (IMU PCB-07, the Hall carrier and the proposed power boards) are the project's own designs, and their boxes are their current definitions.
