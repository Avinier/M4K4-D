# Chassis harness design review — 2026-11-15

This is the static planning and CAD disposition for the open chassis-harness items. `E` means an assumed envelope or cut allowance; nothing here releases a live-cell build.

## Completed now

| Item | Result | Evidence and limit |
|---|---|---|
| W29–W33 signal dress | Five continuous, named outer-cable paths added from the IMU, encoders and drivers to C3 J10-7/3/4/5/6 in `Harness → Chassis wires`. The 15/15 neutral-pose solid check found no printed-part intersections; only intended GH and encoder-connector entry contacts. A cable-to-cable check found no solid overlap with the visible switched, drive or head-supply power leads. | [CAD source](../final/v1/cad/chassis_harness.py) and [check report](../final/v1/cad/check_chassis_harness-report.json); cable diameters and bends are `E`. They do not intersect the printed chassis or bought-module envelopes. The required node per polarity is one AWG16 W05c feed plus four AWG18 outputs: M2, M3, left driver and right driver. | These are **placement allowances**, not selected rated five-wire splices. W05c feeder and output tails still need an electrically explicit fan-out; geometry is not a finished joint. |
| CH-103 balance-fuse fit | Four candidate rows of three assumed insulated Ø4 × 12 mm axial fuse assemblies were checked at the pack-side cell edge and D-095 exit, with J-PK and the print fixed. **0/4 rows cleared all three positions**. Cell-side rows overlapped cell/strap envelopes; exit rows overlapped J-PK, with some also hitting the cell/strap. | Re-run the [CAD study](../final/v1/cad/study_ch103_fuse_fit.py); see its [JSON report](../final/v1/cad/study_ch103_fuse_fit-report.json). This is a bounded search, so it does not prove no solution anywhere in the pack. No fuse bodies were added to the robot CAD. The candidate [Littelfuse 0251001](https://www.littelfuse.com/products/fuses-overcurrent-protection/fuses/axial-radial-thru-hole-fuses/pico-fuses-axial-radial-thru-hole-fuses/251/0251001) has a 1 A, 125 V DC rating, but its selected sleeve, weld/crimp joints and fault behavior are still unproved. |
| W04 rear-panel service plan | The CAD splice axis is (−45.6, −34, Z62..88). The nominal inner end of the 16 mm XT30 body envelope is (−45.5, −8, 64). Straight separation is 26.0 mm installed and 47.6 mm after a 40 mm rearward panel withdrawal. The 100 mm `E` cut therefore leaves at most 52.4 mm for two terminations, bends and a safe rest pose in that example. | The inner body end is a proxy for the actual solder-cup exit; extraction motion and cup strain are unmeasured. Keep W04 as a supported loop in the first mock-up. Add a keyed, polarity-preserving AWG16 service pair if the panel cannot be withdrawn and laid down without loading the XT30 cups or charge splice. Do not freeze a connector PN from this distance screen alone. |
