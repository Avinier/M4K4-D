# Shared PCB footprints

`fh34.pretty/Hirose_FH34SRJ-22S-0.5SH_1x22_P0.50mm.kicad_mod` is the PCB-08/PCB-14 common ZIF footprint, generated by `generate_fh34srj_22.py`. The local +Y direction is the FFC entry; contact 1 is at local +X. Board placement must rotate it so the mouth faces the cassette and must verify pin order with the received cable.

The copper follows Hirose's [FH34 series catalog](https://www.hirose.com/en/product/document?clcode=&documentid=en_FH34_CAT&documenttype=Catalog&lang=en&productname=&series=FH34SRJ), recommended mounting pattern, 22-way row: 0.5 mm pitch, 0.3 × 0.8 mm signal pads, 3.3 mm separation from the 0.4 × 0.8 mm solder tabs, and B/E/F = 10.5/12.1/12.9 mm. Its Fab outline is the closed 13 × 3.8 mm body; its courtyard adds 0.25 mm around the body and 0.4 mm beyond the body at each cable-axis end to include the pads. The open actuator and mated cable are checked separately in CAD.

The signal F.Paste apertures are **0.25 × 0.65 mm**, matching Hirose's recommendation for a **0.1 mm stencil**; the solder-tab apertures match their 0.4 × 0.8 mm copper. KiCad export of F.Cu and F.Paste must show 22 signal apertures and two tab apertures before fabrication. Have the assembler review stencil thickness and aperture reduction. This footprint remains a design input until the received connector and assembly process are checked.

`pcb08.pretty/` holds PCB-08's Micro-Fit 3.0 43045-0621 footprint (stock, with the top silk moved to Fab).

**3D models.** KiCad's released 3D library has no model for the FH34SRJ, the Micro-Fit 43045-0621 or the Mini-SPOX 5267-03A. `generate_connector_models.py` builds detailed, coloured models of all three from the vendors' drawings and writes them beside their footprints:
- `fh34.pretty/…FH34SRJ….step`: beige housing with the 11.5 mm FFC slot, black back-flip actuator, 22 gold contacts and tin solder tabs, 13 × 3.8 × 1.0 mm (FH34 catalogue).
- `pcb08.pretty/…43045-0621….step`: black housing with six polarised cavities, a latch window and ramp, the 1.40 mm front lip, tin pins with 3.18 mm tails and the press-fit clips, 12.65 × 13.67 × 7.40 mm (SD-43045-008).
- `pcb08.pretty/…5267-03A….step`: natural shrouded header with a 1.0 mm floor, 0.7 mm walls, the locking ramp and polarising slot, and round pins 3.5 mm above the floor and below the board (52670001-SD).

Outer sizes follow the drawings; undimensioned details (cavity chamfers, latch, actuator profile) are representative. Use them for review and fit, not tooling, and replace them with vendor STEPs if those become available. `final/v1/cad/check_pcb08_step_fit.py` adds conservative tail envelopes on top.
