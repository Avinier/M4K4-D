**Charger teaching note — why the hot loop controls placement.** The BQ25798 contains the switching FETs; the external inductor and capacitors complete its buck-boost energy-transfer paths. Every switching edge sends a short pulse of current through a capacitor, an internal switch, the SW node/inductor and the return. The small, fast loops radiate and ring if their conductors are spread apart. TI's layout priority is therefore: SYS bypass first, then PMID, then VBUS; keep each 0.1 µF capacitor closest and return it directly on the top layer; place L1 close to SW1/SW2; then BAT caps, bootstrap/REGN bypass, and thermal vias. Keep `BATP` away from both SW nodes. The 0.1 µF loops and SW copper are short/high-frequency geometry; the wide power paths carry the lower-frequency load current. Do not solve one by making all copper areas huge: oversized SW copper increases capacitive coupling and radiated noise. TI's reference layout sends SW1/SW2 through vias under the IC to an inner layer so the small bypass capacitors can remain on the top side.

The TI package is RQM0029A, a 29-pin 4 × 4 mm QFN with exposed thermal/ground pad. Pin mapping to check against the exact datasheet revision at schematic capture: 1 STAT; 2–3 VBUS; 4 BTST1; 5 REGN; 6 D+; 7 D−; 8 VAC2; 9 VAC1; 10 ACDRV2; 11 ACDRV1; 12 QON; 13 CE; 14 SCL; 15 SDA; 16 TS; 17 ILIM_HIZ; 18 BATP; 19 BTST2; 20 PROG; 21 INT; 22–23 BAT; 24 SDRV; 25 SYS; 26 SW2; 27 GND; 28 SW1; 29 PMID; exposed pad GND. Use a project footprint derived from TI's RQM0029A land-pattern drawing if the stock pattern cannot be proved identical. Add a ground thermal-via array under the exposed pad, with via count/diameter and paste segmentation set against the fab rules. Do not route the hot loop by eye around the courtyard: first reserve the capacitor positions, then the two SW escapes and inductor, and check the land pattern/courtyards and solder-mask web against the board's rule file.

**Switching-stage parts and footprint constraints.** Würth 74437346022 is a shielded 7.3 × 6.6 mm inductor, 2.2 µH, ±20%, 10 A saturation current at 10% inductance drop, 7.7 A RMS, 20 mΩ max DCR, approximately 3.0–3.1 mm high (datasheet drawing is governing). It comfortably exceeds the design's 2.7 A estimated inductor peak, but thermal rise at the actual 750 kHz waveform still needs CP-11. Keep L1 beside the QFN's SW1/SW2 pads, with short copper sized for peak current; confine SW copper to the required connection and keep `BATP`, `TS`, and `ILIM_HIZ` outside that switching region. The draft is a **4-layer, 1.6 mm board with 2 oz outer copper and 1 oz inner copper**: this gives the power/thermal copper useful area while enabling the under-IC SW escapes TI shows. It remains a design proposal to validate against the selected fab's stackup and minimum annular/thermal-via geometry before board setup.

`VAC_OVP` is a bench gate, not an assumed pass: CP-OI-06 / CP-04 must read REG10 on the bench before the first charge. The documented reset value 0x85 decodes to 26 V although the field text conflicts; if the measured field is 7 V, stop and raise a decision before charging.
