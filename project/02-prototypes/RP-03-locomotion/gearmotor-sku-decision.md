## Verification and review, 2026-09-26

**Pololu's figures confirmed** on the [#4804 specifications page](https://www.pololu.com/product/4804/specs) (status "Active and Preferred"): 34.014:1; 290 rpm / 0.50 A no-load at 6 V (±20% speed, ±50% current); 240 rpm, 1.3 kg·cm, 1.4 A, 37% at maximum efficiency; 6.8 kg·cm / 6.0 A extrapolated stall; 101 g; 25D × 67L; 4 mm D-shaft 12.5 mm from the faceplate; 1632.67 counts per output revolution. Price US$56.95 each, $52.39 at 5+ (before freight and duty). Pololu's official [dimension diagram](https://www.pololu.com/file/0J1634/25d-metal-gearmotor-dimension-diagram.pdf) and [3D models](https://www.pololu.com/file/0J1647/25d-gearmotor-3d-models.zip) are downloaded to `RP-06-cad/body-chassis/layout-01/references/purchased/pololu_25d/`.

**The Fab.to.Lab page is stale and must not be used as the spec.** It is one family page for #4800–4808. For #4804 it gives 280 rpm, 6.5 kg·cm and a 6.5 A family stall, which are Pololu's older HP 6 V figures, and its text says 211.2 counts per output revolution, copied from another ratio. The page shows no variant stock or price to a fetch. The quote must name "#4804, current revision" and the received label must be checked.

**Linear-motor screen at the operating points (`E`, from Pololu's endpoints, not a measured curve):**

| Case | Result |

| DRV8874 limit set to 3 A | about 0.30 N·m available (0.121 N·m/A above the 0.5 A no-load), 1.37× the 0.220 N·m stall screen; the same limit caps regeneration at the 3 A bus requirement |
| 2.1 A carrier continuous | covers the 1.4 A launch point; a 3 A limit is a short-duration peak and needs the thermal bench check |

**Alternatives screened and rejected:** Pololu LP 6 V 34:1 [#4824](https://www.pololu.com/product/4824/specs) (180 rpm no-load, 150 rpm at 0.051 N·m, fails 159 rpm under load); LP 6 V 20.4:1 [#4823](https://www.pololu.com/product/4823/specs) (2.5 kg·cm / 2.0 A stall, about 160 rpm at 0.110 N·m nominal, no tolerance margin). The 12 V 25D windings run at about half speed on the 2S bus. **#4804 is the only Pololu 25D 6 V variant that clears the screen.** It remains the preferred candidate over NFP `-0685` (tighter speed margin, no orderable variant, no India route) and DFRobot FIT0521 (170 rpm at 0.196 N·m; India supply unverified).

**CAD done (`RP03-CAD-10`, RP-06 `decisions.md`).** Layout 02 now uses the official STEP. The drawing exposed two conflicts the paper review missed: the face M3 holes at R8.5 fall inside the 608 seat (R11.25), and the Ø7 × 2.5 bushing boss stands into the old stub position. The flange boss now closes over the screws with a 2 mm diaphragm (countersunk M3 × 8, 2.85 mm of thread in the tapped faceplate), the stack moves 3 mm outboard, and the motor is clocked so the radial encoder leads exit rearward (clocked up they cut the IMU and battery trunk). 110/110 layout checks pass. The two encoder caps are 5.1 mm apart on the centre plane.

## Paper check at the 6.0 V motor terminals

### Pololu #4804 — current lead (Exa screen 2026-09-26)

[Pololu's exact product page](https://www.pololu.com/product/4804/specs) gives 34.014:1, 290 rpm no-load at 6 V, 240 rpm at its maximum-efficiency point (1.3 kg·cm = 0.127 N·m, 1.4 A), about 6.8 kg·cm (0.667 N·m) and 6.0 A extrapolated stall, 101 g, 25 mm diameter × 67 mm body length, 4 mm D shaft extending 12.5 mm from the face, and 48 CPR on the motor shaft (1632.67 counts/output revolution). Pololu provides an [official drawing and STEP models](https://www.pololu.com/product/4804/resources). Its documented efficient-load point exceeds the 0.110 N·m high-mass launch torque and the 159.2 rpm required for 0.70 m/s at 6 V. That is a paper screen, not a measured operating point at the project's exact torque. Pololu marks no-load speed as typical ±20%, so sample-level loaded-speed proof remains necessary.

The 6.0 A stall specification **exceeds the 5 A Korad bench supply per motor** and the Pololu 4035 DRV8874 carrier's 4.4 A default current limit. Set a verified hardware current limit before first power-up; characterize one motor at a time and do not run or hold a hard stall. The carrier's published continuous capability is about 2.1 A, so the chosen short-duration launch limit and thermal behavior require bench proof. At full 2S voltage, motor-terminal voltage and regeneration policy remain mandatory. The 12.5 mm shaft (rather than the CAD's 9.5 mm placeholder) and 67 mm body require axle/CAD revision, and the mounting pattern must come from the official drawing. Do not substitute obsolete Pololu #2273 for current #4804.

### NFP `-EN-0685 / 35:1` — comparison

NFP's **`-0685` / 35:1 row** gives 242 rpm no-load at 0.15 A, 175 rpm at 0.6 A and 1 kg·cm (0.098 N·m), and 3.9 kg·cm (0.382 N·m) / 2.6 A at stall. These are *one supplier's* values for one winding. The supplier marks reduced no-load speeds ±15%; it does not give a loaded-speed tolerance. Stall is a short-duration endpoint, not a permitted operating state.

On the Ø84 mm wheel, 0.70 m/s requires 159.2 rpm and 0.50 m/s requires 113.7 rpm. The quoted rated point gives 0.770 m/s at the nominal 6.0 V, only **9.9% speed margin** over the 0.70 m/s design point. At the nominal launch requirement of 0.063 N·m per wheel, the rated torque margin is 1.56×; at the high-mass 0.110 N·m corner, rated torque is short (0.89×), although stall torque exceeds the 0.220 N·m screen threshold by 1.74×. This makes the high-mass launch transient and loaded speed **bench gates**, not paper passes. The previous ~211 rpm loaded claim is an estimate at lighter load, not the NFP rated-speed entry. A 15% low no-load unit could miss the 0.70 m/s point after load; the quotation does not rule that out.

At 6 V, two nominal simultaneous stalls would draw 5.2 A, beyond the 5 A Korad bench supply. Power one motor at a time during initial characterization with the supply current limit set before connection. The Pololu 4035 DRV8874 carrier supports approximately 2.1 A continuous per channel and has a 4.4 A default current limit at 5 V `SLEEP`; the design must set and verify a lower hardware limit for this motor. At a full 8.4 V pack, commanded duty and current limiting must keep the **motor terminal** within the validated 6 V operating envelope. A duty ratio near 6/8.4 is only a starting estimate because PWM current and driver drops matter. The separate ≤3 A bus regeneration and zero-regeneration-below-0 °C requirements remain open.

## Supplier and geometry hold

**India availability check, 2026-09-26 (Exa):** No Indian listing for the exact `NFP-JGA25-370-EN-0685 / 35:1 / 242 rpm / encoder` configuration was verified. [IndiaMART](https://www.indiamart.com/proddetail/25mm-dc-metal-encoder-gearmotors-2849495741312.html) identifies a distinct `MOT3001-6V230RPM` encoder SKU but marks it **out of stock**; it provides no matching NFP identity, ratio, loaded-speed or shaft drawing. [ThinkRobotics India](https://thinkrobotics.com/products/25mm-encoder-dc-metal-gearmotors) has a family selector containing 230 rpm, but its visible stock claim applies to whichever variant is selected, so it does not independently confirm stock of that SKU. [Fab.to.Lab](https://www.fabtolab.com/pololu-4800-4801-4802-4803-4804-4805-4806-4807-4808-hp-6v-motor-48-cpr-encoder-25D-mm-metal-gearmotor-dc-brushed) lists the exact Pololu `#4804` variant in India, but its page did not establish variant-specific stock. Its family data show older 280 rpm / 6.5 A figures; use Pololu's current #4804 specifications for design and check the supplied revision. NFP has an **unconfirmed import/special-order path**, not a confirmed India purchase path.

The same NFP encoder product page gives a **10 mm output shaft**, 4 mm diameter and about 110 g mass. Its purchase selector lists 170 rpm but does **not** offer the `-0685` 35:1 row's **242 rpm** no-load variant. Therefore a checkout selection of “6 V / 170 rpm” would buy the slower winding. Keep NFP as a comparison pending a written India-delivery quote for **`NFP-JGA25-370-EN-0685 / 35:1 / 242 rpm no-load at 6 V / encoder`**. Do not relabel a 170/176 rpm motor.

The layout-02 CAD now models the official Pololu 34:1 encoder STEP (`RP03-CAD-10`): 12.5 mm shaft, 66.5 mm body, M3 face screws in a flange diaphragm, 8.5 mm of shaft in the stub. It is the vendor model, not a received article. Before releasing the axle flange or hub, check received samples for shaft protrusion from the faceplate, D-flat length, face-hole spacing and **tapped length (2.85 mm in the STEP)**, bushing-boss height, lead exit and bend room, gearbox radial-load rating and mass. Keep the independent 608 bearing pair so wheel radial load does not depend on an unpublished gearbox limit.

## Receiving and freeze gates

1. Match supplier quotation, invoice, label and two received articles to **Pololu #4804, HP 6 V, 34.014:1 with encoder**; photograph markings. Record any differing current or speed claim before power-up.
2. Measure both articles' envelope, shaft, face pattern, tapped length and encoder interface against the official drawing now in the CAD (`RP03-CAD-10`); update the model and rerun the layout checks if any differ.
3. With one motor at a time on a current-limited supply, measure no-load speed/current at 6.0 V, encoder counts per **wheel** revolution and direction, and a **brief, current-limited** stall or torque-current curve. Do not hold the motor stalled. Confirm the driver limit and 6.0–8.4 V PWM/thermal behaviour.
4. On the representative-mass rig, pass RP-03 P01–P06 and G01/G02/G06, especially loaded 0.70 m/s at the low usable bus, high-mass launch, 0.04 m/s creep, active hold, reversal and acoustic/temperature limits. A miss reopens motor or wheel gearing; it does not change the requirement.

**Lock sequence:** confirm Fab.to.Lab can deliver the exact current Pololu #4804 (two plus spare) to India with a quote and lead time; verify a launch-current limit against the 2.1 A carrier continuous rating and 5 A bench supply (a 3 A limit is the proposed setting); the official-drawing axle geometry is done in CAD (`RP03-CAD-10`). If those conditions fail, compare the DFRobot [FIT0521](https://www.dfrobot.com/product-1617.html) (34:1, 210 rpm no-load, 170 rpm at 0.196 N·m, 3.2 A stall; India supply unverified) and the NFP variant. Choose the orderable part in the RP-03 decision register, then buy/receive samples and complete geometry and bench gates before an installed-part freeze. Until then, **Pololu #4804 is the preferred prototype candidate, not a locked SKU**.
