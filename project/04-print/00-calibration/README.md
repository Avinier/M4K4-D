# Calibration coupons

Small prints that settle fits and settings before the real parts. Each one is
printed on the printer and in the filament its real parts will use: an offset
found on an A1 in PETG does not carry over to the X1E in ASA.

| Coupon | Material, printer | Settles | Feeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tolerance coupon: holes Ø2–Ø8, pins, slots and a dovetail at 0.1 mm steps | PETG on an A1 and a Guider II; ASA and PLA on the X1E | XY hole compensation and sliding clearances | Every part |
| Insert coupon: bosses for each insert article (M3 × 4, M3 × 6, CNC Kitchen M3 × 5.7, M2.5, M2 × 3 OD 3.6) | PETG; then ASA and PLA | Pilot sizes, pull-out, the 20-removal panel cycle (body BOM) | Chassis, body, head |
| 688 ZZ seat coupon: a housing bore at three sizes | PETG | The bearing press fit | CH-051 bearing housing |
| J04 junction coupon: a rail end and a carrier cheek | PETG | The J04 key fit and the axial inserts | CH-001 frame set |
| TPU tyre section: one chevron segment and its clamp | TPU 95A on an A1 | Whether the printed tyre works; the D-005 hold | CH-005 tyres |
| Yaw hub/bearing coupon and a gear pair | PETG | The bearing fit and the gear mesh (D-044) | Body yaw stage |
| CF modulus coupon: a bending bar | PA-CF on the X-CF Pro | ≥ 4.5 GPa along the layers (D-049); needs a UTM or a known-load deflection test | Head yoke legs |
| Colour swatches: a textured-plate face and a fuzzy-skin face per filament | Every filament | Matches against the palette; the finish look | Print guide §4 |

Record each coupon's result, with the printer, filament and settings, in this
folder's `log.md`, and the resulting offsets in the table below.
