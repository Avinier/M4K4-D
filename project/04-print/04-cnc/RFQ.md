# RFQ text — paste one block per part

Each block goes into the platform's notes box for that part, with the part's STEP and
drawing PDF from `upload/<part>/`. The drawing governs; this text says the same thing in a form an
estimator reads first. All dimensions in mm. General tolerance ISO 2768-m.

**Common note (route A orders):** *Drawings attached for every part: please quote to
the drawing, not the instant price. Parts have ISO 286 fits and tapped holes. As
machined, no plating, deburred; light oil on steel. Please confirm fits are accepted
and quote lead time to [city].*

---

## CH-012 — Axle stub (route A)

```
CH-012 AXLE STUB, rev A. Qty 3. EN8 (080M40) or C45 steel, as turned, no plating, light oil.
Fits: Ø8 k5 (8.001/8.007) bearing seats; Ø10 g6 (9.986/9.995) spigot; Ø4.05 H8 (4.050/4.068) bore 9 deep from the inboard end.
Turn Ø8, Ø10 and the Ø4.05 bore in one setup: concentric within 0.02. Shoulder face and both flange faces square to the axis within 0.02.
Shoulder corner sharp or undercut (no fillet over R0.15): bearings seat on it. Inboard end chamfer 0.3x45; break other edges 0.2.
Threads: 3x M3x0.5-6H through the Ø21 flange on Ø16 PCD; M3x0.5-6H axial in the spigot end, 8 deep (drill Ø2.5 x 9.5);
1x radial M3x0.5-6H through one wall into the bore (drill Ø2.5). Mass ~13 g each.
```

## HD-004 — Roll hub collar (route A)

```
HD-004 ROLL HUB COLLAR, rev A. Qty 2. 6061-T6 or 6082-T6 aluminium, deburred, no finish.
Ø20 x 4 flange, Ø12 shaft, overall 12.5. Bore Ø6 H7 (6.000/6.012) through, 0.3 chamfer both ends.
Turn Ø20, Ø12, the bore and the flange (horn) face in one setup; face square to the bore within 0.02.
Threads: 4x M2x0.4-6H through the flange on Ø14 PCD (must match a supplied 25T servo horn; drawing shows positions);
2x radial M3x0.5-6H into the bore at 5.8 and 9.7 from the flange face (drill Ø2.5). Break edges 0.2. Mass ~4.8 g each.
```

## CH-013 — Ballast bar (route A with shop copper, or route B with the builder's offcut)

```
CH-013 BALLAST BAR, rev A. Qty 1. C110 / ETP copper (Cu >= 99.9 %). Do not substitute brass or steel: the part is ballast and its mass matters.
Mill all six faces to 60 x 9 x 19 (from ~10 x 20 flat). Top face (the 60 x 9 face with the holes) flat within 0.1. Break all edges 0.3.
2x M3x0.5-6H from the top face, 40 ±0.1 apart, centred on the 9 mm width, 10 from each end; thread 8 deep, drill Ø2.5 x 9, countersink 0.3.
Copper is gummy: sharp spiral-point or forming tap with cutting oil. Nominal mass 90.8 g: please weigh and report.
```

## BO-046 (fixed half) — Fixed pinion half (route A: wire EDM or fibre laser)

```
BO-046 FIXED PINION HALF, rev A. Qty 2. 7075-T6 aluminium plate 2.0 ±0.05 (6061-T6 acceptable), flat within 0.03. No finish.
Cut the profile from the attached DXF (mm, layer CUT) by wire EDM (preferred) or fibre laser, or generate the gear from:
spur gear m 1, z 37, 20° pressure angle, x 0, tip Ø39.0, root Ø34.5, teeth thinned for 0.15 backlash: span over 5 teeth 13.732 -0.03/+0.
Bore Ø5 H7 (5.000/5.012) with a flat 4.55 +0.03/0 across. Peg hole Ø2 H7 through at R10 on the flat's centre line, opposite the flat.
Break tooth edges 0.3 on the bottom face, 0.1 on the top; deburr the bore. Mass ~5.8 g each.
```

## HD-009 — Pitch pivot pin (route B, builder-supplied hardened rod)

```
HD-009 PITCH PIVOT PIN, rev A. Qty 2. From the supplied Ø6 h6 hardened EN-31 rod (~60 HRC). Do not turn or polish the Ø6.

```

## HD-034 — Roll spindle (route B, the same hardened rod)

```
HD-034 ROLL SPINDLE, rev A. Qty 2. From the supplied Ø6 h6 hardened EN-31 rod. Do not turn or polish the Ø6.
Cut to 44.4 ±0.1, ends square within 0.05, chamfer both ends 0.3x45.
Grind two flats, each 5.5 +0/-0.05 across (0.5 deep), parallel to the axis within 0.03:
  front flat 9.8 long from the front end (+0.3/-0; it must not be shorter);
  rear flat 8.6 ±0.3 long from the rear end, at 90° ±2° to the front flat:
  seen from the rear end with the front flat at 12 o'clock, the rear flat is at 9 o'clock.
Bearing journals 11.5-16.5 and 29.1-34.1 from the rear end must stay round and unmarked (they run in 696 bearings).
```
